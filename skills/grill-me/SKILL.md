> 所有面向用户的输出使用**简体中文**。技术术语（代码、路径、CLI 命令、专有名词）保持英文。

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead. **Read `.planning/codebase/STRUCTURE.md` and `.planning/graphs/GRAPH_REPORT.md` first if they exist** — they're pre-computed maps; don't re-walk what's already mapped.
